Outdoors

Pool & Gardens

The centrepiece of Casa Fiordalisi is its stunning infinity pool, set within extensive private gardens with panoramic views across the Valdichiana valley towards Cortona. Sun loungers, a stone terrace, and total privacy make this the ideal place to spend long Tuscan days and warm evenings.

Ground Floor

Living Room

At the heart of the house is a generous open-plan living room with original terracotta floors, exposed timber beams, and a grand stone fireplace with wood-burning stove. Comfortable sofas, antique furniture, and arched French doors opening to the garden create a space that is both characterful and deeply relaxed.

Ground Floor

Dining Room & Kitchen

The dining area flows naturally from the living room, centred around a large table perfect for long Italian meals together. The fully equipped kitchen adjoins the space, making it easy to cook and socialise at the same time. An outdoor terrace just beyond provides the ideal setting for al fresco dinners under the Tuscan sky.

Upstairs · Bedroom 1

Master Bedroom with Terrace

The master bedroom is a generous, light-filled room upstairs with a beautiful wrought-iron double bed, exposed beams, terracotta floors, and antique Tuscan furnishings. French doors lead directly onto a private terrace — the perfect spot for a morning coffee with views over the garden. This room has its own en-suite bathroom.

Upstairs · Bedroom 2

Double Bedroom

A beautiful double bedroom featuring an exposed stone wall, traditional rattan-headboard bed, terracotta floors, and antique wooden furniture. The room is bright and airy with countryside views, and shares the upstairs bathroom with the loggia bedroom.

Upstairs · Bedroom 3

Double Bedroom with Loggia

A charming double room with beautiful floral linen, a wrought-iron bed, and large doors leading onto a loggia — a covered outdoor space perfect for sitting out in the shade. Light, airy and full of character, this room shares the upstairs bathroom.

Upstairs · Bedroom 4

Bedroom with Bunkbeds & Mezzanine

A wonderfully characterful room ideal for children or younger guests. Bunkbeds sit beneath an exposed stone wall and terracotta-tiled ceiling, with a feature wrought-iron staircase leading up to a mezzanine level — a private sleeping platform that kids absolutely love. A travel cot is also available. Shares the upstairs bathroom.

Bathrooms & Extras

Casa Fiordalisi has three bathrooms in total: an en-suite for the master bedroom, a shared upstairs bathroom for the other three bedrooms, and a ground floor WC. The property also includes a dedicated outdoor games barn with table tennis — a firm favourite with families.
