Cycling & Walks on the Sentiero della Bonifica

✍ Casa Fiordalisi📅 July 2024⏱ 1 min read📍 Accessible from the house

The Sentiero della Bonifica is a reclamation path in the Valdichiana between Arezzo and Chiusi. It ambles along the Canale Maestro della Chiana, stretching approximately 60 kilometres from Arezzo to Chiusi.

Originally used for canal and lock maintenance, the path is completely flat — making it perfectly suited for family-friendly walks or cycling at any pace. And it can be accessed directly from the house.

Essential Info

  • Distance60km total (Arezzo to Chiusi)
  • TerrainCompletely flat — ideal for all abilities
  • AccessDirectly accessible from Casa Fiordalisi
  • Best forFamilies, leisurely cycling, long walks
  • BikesHire available in Castiglion Fiorentino

A rare Tuscan path that requires no hills — just open countryside, canal reflections, and the sound of birdsong all the way to Chiusi.

Whether you choose to walk a short section from the house or cycle the full length over a day, the Sentiero della Bonifica offers a peaceful, car-free way to experience the Valdichiana landscape at its most unhurried.
