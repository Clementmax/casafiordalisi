The Property

Casa Fiordalisi

Casa Fiordalisi is a stunning four-bedroom renovated farmhouse featuring a fabulous infinity pool and breathtaking views across the Valdichiana valley. The traditional stone house blends authentic Tuscan character — exposed beams, terracotta floors, a grand stone fireplace — with a modern, light-filled open-plan layout.

Private, well-equipped, and family-friendly, the gardens and pool area offer panoramic vistas extending to Cortona and Castiglion Fiorentino. The perfect base to explore the wonders of Tuscany and Umbria at your own pace.

Situated between the historic towns of Cortona and Siena, and close to the A1 motorway, Casa Fiordalisi puts Renaissance culture, celebrated local wines, and the UNESCO-listed Val d'Orcia all within easy reach.
